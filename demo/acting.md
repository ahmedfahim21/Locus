# Locus demo: acting scripts

What the two of you say and do on camera. Screen recordings, title cards, the diagram and the edit are built on top of this (see `plan.md` section 7 for the shot list).

Locus's lines below are what it actually says on this home today. Every rider line was tested against the matcher; say it as written, then ad-lib around it once it works.

## Cast and setup

- Fahim: the recipient. At home, laptop open on locus.ahmedfahim.dev, his own phone nearby.
- Rider: a teammate in a delivery jacket (or plain T-shirt and a delivery bag), on a scooter with a phone mount. His phone is the one Locus calls.
- Camera: helmet or chest cam on the rider (one long take per call), plus a second phone filming the mounted phone's screen. Fahim's laptop screen is recorded separately.
- Route: Dallas Centre road → ITC Kohenur → Knowledge City → Qualcomm building (the door).

Before rolling:
- Turn on "Expecting a delivery" for the dev home (it expires; it was off on 2026-11-22).
- On the try-a-call screen, put the rider's number (the teammate's phone, not Fahim's) in "Call the rider".
- Do one full dry run on the route. Record in a quiet lane first; road noise is the biggest risk.
- The rider speaks after Locus finishes, one sentence at a time, mouth close to the phone.

## Scene 1: the problem (no Locus)

Junction near Dallas Centre. Rider stopped, helmet on, calls the customer. Fahim answers mid-task (on a laptop call, or cooking), distracted.

> Rider: Hello sir, Swiggy se. Main aa gaya hoon area mein, aapka ghar kidhar hai?
>
> Fahim: Haan haan, woh… ITC ke peeche se aaiye, phir ek left lena hai…
>
> Rider: Kaunsa left, sir? Yahan teen left hain.
>
> Fahim: Woh… ek minute, main location bhejta hoon.

Rider sighs, looks at his phone. Cut.

Both: play it tired, not comic. This is the call everyone recognises.

## Scene 2: setup (Fahim, once)

Mostly screen recording. Film Fahim only for two short inserts:
- Fahim recording the walk video: phone held up, walking from the main road to the gate, 30 seconds, no talking.
- Fahim at the laptop ticking the suggested landmarks, a small nod when the 3D street shows the gate.

No lines. Keep it calm: this happens once.

## Scene 3: the call (one take)

Rider is at the start of the route, near ITC Kohenur, about 600 m from the door. Fahim taps Call the rider. The rider's phone rings on the mount; he answers on speaker.

> Locus: Namaste, main ek AI assistant hoon, aur raasta batane ke liye yeh call transcribe ho rahi hai. Delivery kiske liye hai, aur kaunse app se?
>
> Rider: Fahim ke liye, Swiggy se.
>
> Locus: Aap abhi kahan hain? Aas paas koi dukaan ya landmark bataiye.
>
> Rider: ITC Kohenur ke paas hoon.
>
> Locus: ITC Kohenur se Knowledge City ki taraf aaiye.

Rider rides on. Cut on the move, pick up as Knowledge City comes into view.

> Rider: Knowledge City dikh raha hai.
>
> Locus: Knowledge City ke baad Qualcomm building tak seedha aaiye.

Rider rides to the gate and stops.

> Rider: Qualcomm ke saamne hoon.
>
> Locus: Bas, aap pahunch gaye. Shukriya!

Call ends. Rider takes the bag off, small smile. Hold two seconds.

Fahim, at home: glances at the laptop, sees "Arrived", goes back to what he was doing. He never picked up the phone; that's the point.

If Locus asks again ("Ek baar phir bataiye…" or "Maaf kijiye, samajh nahi aaya…"), repeat the same line once, slower. If it still misses, cut and restart the take.

## Scene 4: when Locus can't place him (separate take)

Rider somewhere off the route. New call, same opening and screening as Scene 3 (shorten in the edit).

> Locus: Aap abhi kahan hain? Aas paas koi dukaan ya landmark bataiye.
>
> Rider: Ek chai ki dukaan ke paas khada hoon.
>
> Locus: Maaf kijiye, samajh nahi aaya. Aapko aas paas aur kya dikh raha hai?
>
> Rider: Pata nahi bhaiya, kahin galat aa gaya hoon.
>
> Locus: Main unhe call kar raha hoon, line par rahiye.

Fahim's phone rings at home. He picks up.

> Locus: Aapko jod raha hoon.
>
> Fahim: Haan bhaiya, chai wale ke saamne se seedha aaiye, main gate pe aata hoon.
>
> Rider: Theek hai sir.

Do not use "Mindspace" here: Locus still places it at Knowledge City.

## Scene 5: "don't record me" (separate short take)

During guiding, after "Aap abhi kahan hain?":

> Rider: Record mat karo.
>
> Locus: Theek hai, ab se kuch record nahi hoga, aur ab tak ka bhi mita diya. Aap abhi kahan hain? Aas paas koi dukaan ya landmark bataiye.

Say "record mat karo", not "recording mat karo": the first is the tested phrase. On Fahim's screen the transcript blanks; film that.

## Scene 6: close

Rider hands over the bag at the gate. Fahim (or a family member) takes it. No dialogue, or just:

> Fahim: Thank you bhaiya.
>
> Rider: Sir, aaram se mil gaya.
